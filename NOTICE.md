# Public Use Notice

`vs-ir-eval` is a public mentoring aid for preparing startup IR decks and business plans. It does not generate investment advice, suitability judgments, investment commitments, pass/fail decisions, or official VentureSquare investment decisions.

## Do Not Enter

- Confidential IR decks or full unpublished pitch materials
- Personal information
- Contracts and source financial documents
- Term sheets, cap tables, shareholder lists, or investor-specific terms
- Trade secrets or core technical details before patent filing
- Non-public customer names, revenue accounts, partner names, or other sensitive business information

## How To Read Outputs

- Scores are only an aid for organizing evidence. They are not investment decisions.
- The grade bands `Excellent / Strong / Moderate / Weak / Needs Work` indicate mentoring priority.
- `Founder claim` means information submitted by the founder or user.
- `Externally verified fact` means information checked against public sources.
- `Unverified` or `Not performed (no browsing tool)` items should not be cited as facts.
- VCS investor or fund candidates are outreach examples or search guidance, not recommendations or guarantees of investment interest.

## Recommended Modes

- Founder self-check: `Coaching mode`
- Investor pre-screening memo: `Screening mode`
- Long diagnostic document: `Full report mode`

Final judgment must be made by people through source materials, founder interviews, customer validation, financial/legal/technical due diligence, and responsible professional review.
